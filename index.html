About Me

Researcher. Educator. Explorer.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher working in fluid dynamics and heat & mass transfer. My research spans hydrodynamic and thermal instability, double and triple diffusive convection, magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), hybrid nanofluids, porous media, and bio-convection.

Using linear and weakly nonlinear stability analysis alongside numerical methods, I investigate how modulated boundaries, internal heat sources, magnetic fields, and chemical reactions shape transport phenomena, with applications from thermal engineering to biological systems.

Research

Areas of Interest

🌊

Hydrodynamic & Thermal Instability

Linear and weakly nonlinear instability analysis of double and triple diffusive convection under internal heat sources, gravity, and boundary modulations.

🧲

MHD & Magneto-Convection

Magnetohydrodynamic flows with Hall currents, radial magnetic fields, and magneto-convection in Newtonian and non-Newtonian (Williamson, Walter-B) fluids.

🔥

Nanofluids & Bio-Convection

Heat and mass transfer in hybrid nanofluids (Cu–Al₂O₃, MoS₂–SiO₂), porous media, and bio-convection dynamics of microorganisms in buoyant fluids.
